# 🔍 Key Findings — Movie Industry Analysis

## 📊 Overall Performance

- The dataset contains **1,000 movies**.
- The overall **average movie rating is 6.94/10**.
- Total gross revenue is approximately **$182.89 billion** based on the dataset's revenue values in millions.
- Average movie runtime is approximately **121 minutes**.

---

## 📈 Revenue & Rating by Decade

- The **2010s generated the highest total revenue**, at approximately **$64,248.27 million**.
- The **2000s generated approximately $61,858.89 million**, making it the second-highest revenue decade.
- Average ratings were highest in the **1970s (8.20)**.
- Average ratings declined from the earlier decades and reached their lowest level in the **2010s (6.82)** before slightly increasing to **6.88 in the 2020s**.
- The analysis suggests that higher revenue in recent decades does not necessarily correspond to higher average movie ratings.

---

## 🎭 Revenue by Genre

The highest-revenue genres were:

1. **Thriller — $24,841.94M**
2. **Sci-Fi — $23,918.64M**
3. **Mystery — $22,303.37M**
4. **Animation — $21,808.89M**
5. **Action — $21,639.74M**

- **Thriller** generated the highest total revenue among the analyzed genres.
- **Biography** generated the lowest total revenue at approximately **$15,565.89M**.

> **Note:** Genre revenue totals exceed the overall movie revenue total because a movie can belong to multiple genres. Therefore, genre-level revenue should not be summed to calculate total movie revenue.

## 🔞 Certificate vs Revenue

The highest total revenue by certificate was:

1. **PG-13 — $63,905.12M**
2. **PG — $47,567.06M**
3. **R — $46,583.13M**
4. **G — $9,694.04M**
5. **Not Rated — $8,687.83M**
6. **NC-17 — $6,456.52M**

- **PG-13 movies generated the highest total revenue** in the dataset.
- PG and R-rated movies also contributed significantly to overall revenue.

---

## 📊 Votes vs Rating — Correlation Analysis

- The correlation coefficient between **audience votes and movie rating is approximately 0.107**.
- This indicates a **very weak positive relationship** between the number of votes and movie ratings.
- In this dataset, a higher number of audience votes does **not strongly predict a higher movie rating**.

---

## 💡 Business & Analytical Takeaways

### 1. Revenue increased significantly in recent decades
The 2000s and 2010s account for the largest share of the analyzed revenue, with the 2010s being the highest.

### 2. Revenue and ratings tell different stories
Although recent decades generated substantially higher revenue, their average ratings were lower than several earlier decades.

### 3. Thriller and Sci-Fi performed strongly
These genres ranked among the highest in total revenue, indicating strong commercial performance within the dataset.

### 4. PG-13 movies had the strongest revenue performance
PG-13 had the highest total revenue among the certificate categories analyzed.

### 5. Votes have little relationship with ratings
The low correlation of **0.107** indicates that popularity measured through vote volume is not strongly associated with rating scores.

---

## ⚠️ Data Considerations

- Revenue values are represented in **millions**.
- Genre-level revenue can overlap because individual movies may have multiple genres.
- Director rankings should be interpreted alongside the number of movies associated with each director.
- Correlation shows association, not causation.
